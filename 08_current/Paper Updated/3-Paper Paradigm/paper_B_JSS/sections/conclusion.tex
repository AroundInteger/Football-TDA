\section{Conclusion}

Topological persistence offers football analysts a continuous,
automatically computable measure of tactical state that tracks real
match events coherently and is not redundant with the geometric
descriptors already in common use. It has not yet been shown to improve
predictive models at the sample size available here, a question the
field is well placed to revisit as larger tracking datasets become
available. For now, its clearest practical value lies in monitoring
team shape at scale rather than in forecasting it.
