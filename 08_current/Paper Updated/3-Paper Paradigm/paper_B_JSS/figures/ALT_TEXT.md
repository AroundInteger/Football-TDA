# Figure alt text (Journal of Sports Sciences)

JSS adds AI-generated alt text by default. Prefer these author-written
descriptions for the three argument-carrying figures.

## fig_event_correlation.pdf (`fig:events`)

Bar chart of mean change in H1 total persistence after versus before each
match event type, shown separately for the individual scale (grey bars)
and the tactical scale (dark bars). Event types are ordered from the
strongest negative change (disruption of team shape) on the left to the
strongest positive change (organisation) on the right. Significance stars
mark FDR-corrected Mann–Whitney tests. The plot shows that disruptive
events reduce persistence and build-up or set-play events increase it,
at both scales.

## fig_bilateral_timeseries.pdf (`fig:bilateral`)

Line plot of tactical-scale H1 total persistence across 150 uniformly
sampled frames of the primary match. Three series: home team (blue),
away team (orange), and the merged 22-player reference (grey). The home
and away series rise and fall largely out of step with each other,
illustrating weak frame-level coupling (Spearman ρ = 0.037).

## fig_roc_overlay.pdf (`fig:roc`)

Receiver operating characteristic curves for build-up phase
classification under match-grouped cross-validation. The dashed curve
uses four geometric descriptors only (AUC = 0.693). The solid curve adds
tactical-scale H1 total persistence (AUC = 0.687). The two curves nearly
overlap, showing no predictive gain from the topological feature at the
present sample size.
