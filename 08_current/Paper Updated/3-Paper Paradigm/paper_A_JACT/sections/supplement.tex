\section*{Supplementary note: temporal autocorrelation}
\label{sec:acf}

Headline $H_0$ and $H_1$ tables use the uniform 150-frame rule of
Section~2.1, not a lag chosen from the autocorrelation function.
When available, Figure~\ref{fig:acf} is a diagnostic on the primary
match: topological summaries computed on complete frames at the native
rate, then thinned to 1~Hz, with Pearson autocorrelation plotted against
lag. Vertical guides mark 10~s, 20~s, and the operational stride
($\approx 29$~s). The figure supports the claim that the operational
stride is conservative; it is not used to select frames. Generate with
\texttt{pipeline/steps/09\_acf\_supplement.py}.
