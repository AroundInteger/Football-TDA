\section{Conclusion}

By construction, each diagram is computed on the centroids of one
organisational level. On this corpus the named group-count targets
are met at $2.75$~m, $11.75$~m, and $23.0$~m, and all ten match
means lie in the acceptance bands. On the primary match, every
finite $H_1$ bar has a simple-cycle representative, present at birth
by construction. Tactical loops exceed the group-count-matched null
of Section~\ref{sec:stats} by $9.7$ percentage points
(Section~\ref{sec:h1detection}). The two loop-bearing levels are
associated at Spearman $\rho=0.234$, co-occur one-sidedly, and have
bottleneck distance on the order of the tactical mean bar
(Section~\ref{sec:complementarity}). Football was the measurement
setting. Applying the pipeline to another bounded competitive system
requires re-deriving the interaction lengths, which is not tested
here.
