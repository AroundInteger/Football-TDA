# Abstract structure guide — TDA Football Papers A & B

**Purpose:** Reusable template for Paper A (JACT) and Paper B (JSS) abstracts and for revising abstracts in review sessions.

**Cross-reference:** `review-tda-football-papers.md` §7; `working_foundations.md` §2, §5.

---

## Paper A (JACT) — methodological abstract

**Venue constraint:** 150–250 words; no equations; author–year references not in abstract.

| Part | Rhetorical job | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Problem** | Single-parameter PH conflates organisational levels in multi-agent point clouds | 1–2 sentences |
| **2. Approach** | Two contributions: domain-informed clustering at validated δ; adaptive filtration for consistent H₁ | 2–3 sentences |
| **3. Validation** | Ten matches; three H₀ regimes; two H₁ regimes; robustness across δ and percentile | 3–4 sentences with key numbers |
| **4. Supporting evidence** | Complementarity (ρ); cycle recovery; minimal event correlation as noise check | 1–2 sentences |
| **5. Scope** | Domain-agnostic framework; football as testbed | 1 sentence |

**Sentence-level rules (Paper A house style):**
- One claim per sentence
- No em dashes
- Quantitative results include uncertainty where reported in main text (±, CI)
- Do not name Paper B or football-specific practitioner implications

**Checklist before sign-off:**
- [ ] Word count 150–250
- [ ] No equations or undefined H₀/H₁ jargon without one plain-language gloss
- [ ] Numbers match Results tables
- [ ] Closing sentence states domain-agnostic intent

## Paper B (JSS) — application abstract

**Venue constraint:** Unstructured; ~200–250 words; numbers minimal in prose (headline findings only).

| Part | Rhetorical job | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Gap** | Ring-like team-shape structures lack quantitative definitions; geometric descriptors miss loops | 2 sentences |
| **2. Method hook** | Multi-scale PH method validated in companion paper; this paper tests football utility | 1–2 sentences |
| **3. Findings** | Event tracking; non-redundancy vs geometry; bilateral independence; predictive null | 4–5 sentences, plain language |
| **4. Implication** | Monitoring value now; prediction requires larger sample | 1–2 sentences |

**Sentence-level rules (Paper B house style):**
- No inline p-values or ρ in abstract (reserve for tables)
- No rhetorical questions
- Honest predictive limitation in final sentences
- Cite companion paper by name or “companion paper” — update to arXiv when available

**Checklist before sign-off:**
- [ ] Does not re-derive methodology
- [ ] Four study aims reflected in findings paragraph
- [ ] Predictive null framed as boundary condition, not failure
- [ ] Word count proportionate (~200–250)

## Review flags

Flag as **[SIGNIFICANT]** in submission drafts if:
- Paper A abstract exceeds 250 words or includes equations
- Paper B abstract carries dense statistics better placed in tables
- Either abstract overclaims relative to locked claims in `working_foundations.md` §1a
- Paper A abstract mentions JSS / football analytics audience
- Paper B abstract headlines methodology validation instead of football findings
