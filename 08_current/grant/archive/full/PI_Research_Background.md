**Programme through-line.** Data-driven mathematical methods that recover **ensemble organisation from individual-agent measurements**: (i) large cellular populations (equation-free / stochastic ensemble models); (ii) spatially structured biological networks (fractal and spectral geometry of fibrin clots); (iii) discrete-object tracking at scale; (iv) high-frequency sport measurement and load analytics (rugby, football, swimming, Olympic sport); (v) this application — multi-scale persistent homology of competitive 22-agent point clouds. Sport is an established PI measurement domain since 2020; this grant is the mathematical deepening, not a first move into sport.
