**15/10/2026**

EPSRC Mathematical Sciences Small Grants  
UK Research and Innovation

**Re:** Letter of support for application APP98322  
**Project:** Multi-scale persistent homology for competitive collective systems: a professional-football testbed  
**Project Lead:** Dr Rowan Brown, Swansea University

Dear Sir or Madam,

Swansea City Association Football Club (Swansea City AFC) is pleased to support Dr Rowan Brown's application to the EPSRC Mathematical Sciences Small Grants scheme.

**Commitment.** If the award is made, Swansea City AFC will support the twelve-month project as a project partner. Support is in kind. There is no cash contribution. The club will provide access to Championship tracking data and tactical labels through its commercial data agreement, and will take part in scheduled co-development reviews with the university team. Should funding be awarded, we will enter into a formal collaboration agreement with Swansea University as required for UKRI audit.

**Value to Swansea City AFC.** The project will deliver checkable structural measures of pressing, formation gaps and defensive-line organisation, developed against our matches and footage. These outputs are not available from conventional geometric descriptors such as team length, width or convex-hull area. They will give our performance and analysis staff quantifiable, scale-specific summaries of collective organisation that can be checked against the match.

**Value Swansea City AFC brings.** The club brings the production tracking feed required for both project objectives, formation and phase-of-play labels, and coach- and analyst-facing checks that the mathematical summaries name real organisational states. We also bring an established research relationship with Swansea University, including co-investment in doctoral training, which supports efficient delivery within the twelve-month award.

We confirm that this partnership is relevant to our performance analysis programme and that we are willing to act as the sole industry project partner for the application named above.

Yours faithfully,
