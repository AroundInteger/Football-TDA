## Contributions to the Generation of New Ideas, Tools, Methodologies, or Knowledge

The Project Lead (PL), Dr Rowan Brown, is Senior Lecturer in Biomedical Engineering at Swansea University (MPhys, first class, 2000; PhD, 2004). For more than fifteen years the PL has developed data-driven methods that recover ensemble organisation from individual-agent measurements, from cellular populations and spatial biological networks to competitive multi-agent systems in professional sport. This Small Grant poses the same microstate-to-macrostate question for a 22-player point cloud using multi-scale persistent homology. The PL delivered a 12-month EPSRC First Grant (EP/M000621/1, £96,009) on ensemble behaviour, giving direct precedent for a time-boxed award at this scale.

**Prior funded research (selected).** As PI: EPSRC Mobility Fellowship (EP/G037841/1); the First Grant above with GE Healthcare and Professor Tamás Vicsek; rugby head-load measurement (Sports and Wellbeing Analytics / Welsh Government, 2020/21); Swim Wales training-load analytics (2024/25). As Co-I: Office of Naval Research Global hetero-swarm robotics (2021/22); Welsh Institute of Performance Science; English Institute of Sport data-science applications. Investigator work on nanoparticle tracking (EP/H008683/1) and clot-network geometry (EP/L024799/1) established high-volume spatial time-series pipelines. First-author work in *Physica D* (2010); collaborative outputs in *Nature Nanotechnology* (2011), *Nature Methods* (2014) and *Nature Communications* (2019).

**Most relevant prior outputs (PL)**:

1. Brown MR, et al. Flow-based cytometric analysis of cell cycle via simulated cell populations. *PLoS Computational Biology* 2010 — ensemble models from individual measurements.
2. Brown MR, et al. Fractal discrimination of random fractal aggregates and its application in biomarker analysis for blood coagulation. *Chaos, Solitons & Fractals* 2012 — geometric spatial networks.
3. Brown R, Scott G, Kilduff L. Relative Advantage: quantifying performance in noisy competitive settings. Preprint 2025 — competitive systems as a measurement domain.

**Technical innovations (this programme):** measurement-aware clustering for spatial TDA; adaptive multi-scale H1 filtration; closed-cycle H1 realisation; temporal persistence landscapes. The ten-match validation in Vision and Approach is PL-led foundation for this award; code and parameters feed the Month-2 gate. Python implementation (Ripser, GUDHI, giotto-tda) runs at <2 s per frame on Supercomputing Wales; open-source release is planned.

## Development of Others and Maintenance of Effective Working Relationships

**Doctoral supervision and partnerships:** The PL first-supervises six full-time doctoral students on Championship match-outcome analytics (EPSRC Doctoral Training Partnership with Swansea City AFC, club part-funded), UK Sports Institute (UKSI) analytics, spatiotemporal rugby and football analytics, swimming performance prediction, head-acceleration metrology, and Team INEOS digital twins; and second-supervises responsible AI in government security operations. The EPSRC–SCAFC co-investment is the funded precedent for this grant's partnership; Declan O'Hara's Championship match-outcome doctorate is the closest applied sibling to the season-scale topology pipeline. An EPSRC CASE studentship with Ospreys Rugby produced peer-reviewed outputs with club co-authorship. Graduates have moved into elite-sport analytics roles in professional rugby union and Premier League football.

**Research Associate (RA) development:** The RA (1.0 FTE, Months 5–10) receives training in topological data analysis (TDA), inference under dependence, and scientific programming, with co-authorship and Standard Grant drafting exposure at Months 9–10. Weekly PL meetings, monthly reviews, and scheduled guidance from Project co-lead (PcL) Dr Nelly Villamizar (Mathematics) cover landscape well-posedness and stability for Objective 2 (O2).

**RA deliverables (D1–D3):** **D1 (Month 7):** reproducible H0 and H1 barcode store for ~540 Championship matches with provenance hashes (O1 gate). **D2 (Month 8):** persistence landscapes, Fréchet means, and cumulative sum (CUSUM) module at both scales (O2); comparison-geometry results and T1/T2 statements follow at Month 9 against the Month-2 Open Science Framework (OSF) pre-registration. **D3 (Month 10):** documented pipeline, figures and prose for the season paper and Month-12 Standard Grant evidence pack. Months 1–4 are PL-led (runnable pipeline and Month-2 gate) so D1–D3 are scale-up and proof work, not greenfield design.

**Equality, diversity and inclusion:** RA recruitment follows Swansea EDI practice (anonymised shortlisting, diverse panel, outreach via Piscopia, BWM, London Mathematical Society (LMS)).

**Resources and delivery (JeS):** IPA 799 v2 totals £95,188 fEC (£76,150 at 80%), 1 Mar 2027–29 Feb 2028: PL 0.10 FTE and PcL Villamizar 0.05 FTE (12 months); RA 1.0 FTE Months 5–10 (1 Jul–31 Dec 2027). Months 1–4 are PL-led; RA spend scales a gated pipeline (D1–D3). Supercomputing Wales and SCAFC–StatsBomb data are in-kind; no equipment (Resources and Costs).

## Contributions to the Wider Research and Innovation Community

**Academic and open science:** Methodological publications, SIAM and applied-topology dissemination, and open-source TDA software. Supervised work includes peer-reviewed rugby analytics (*Journal of Science and Medicine in Sport* and *International Journal of Sports Physiology and Performance*, 2023).

**Innovation partnerships:** Swansea City AFC, UKSI, Sport Wales (WIPS), Team INEOS, and the hetero-swarm programme evidence multi-agent work beyond football.

**Cross-domain relevance:** The lens transfers to other bounded competitive systems with validated scales; follow-on targets include tumour–immune competition and competitive logistics with autonomous fleets. Public engagement uses topology visualisations through sport.

## Contributions to Broader Research or Innovation Users and Towards Wider Societal Benefit

**Mathematical sciences:** Validated multi-scale workflows, efficient season-scale computation, and teaching materials. Hosted in the Zienkiewicz Institute with Supercomputing Wales and secure storage.

**Broader users and societal benefit:** Wider users include organisations seeking coordination metrics in multi-agent settings (robotics, logistics, crowd safety). Societal benefits include safer crowded-space management, sports-analytics economic value, and public inspiration through accessible mathematics. The PL previously translated incipient-clot fractal dimension into a clinically interpretable venous thromboembolism biomarker. That translation is direct precedent for practitioner-facing topological summaries on this award, co-developed with Swansea City AFC and StatsBomb.
