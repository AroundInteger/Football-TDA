## Justification (timeline)

**Research Associate (Months 2–10).** Specialist computational post for O1 and O2.

- **O1 (RA from Month 2 to Month 9):** concurrent pipeline on Supercomputing Wales; 20-match cutoff gate at Month 2; full-season barcode database at Month 7 (D1). Comparison-geometry *results* (Months 6–9) are PI-led; the RA maintains the library they use.
- **O2 (Months 4–10):** landscape module at Month 8 (D2); T1/T2 statements and calibrated change-point outputs at Month 9; results finalised for the season paper at Month 10.
- **Evidence pack:** RA handover at Month 10 (D3: results finalised). The Month-12 Standard Grant pack is PI.

**PI (0.2 FTE, Months 1–12).** Month-1 pipeline and 20-match gate design; O1 comparison geometry (Months 6–9); O2 theorem work and change-point calibration (Months 4–9) with RA implementation; Month-10 results finalisation; Month-12 evidence pack; publications (methodology Months 1–2; full-season results Month 11; football-analytics paper Month 12); partnership management and Month-11 practitioner outputs.

**Co-I Kilduff.** Tactical interpretation and label quality for O1 (Cohen’s κ, formation states) and O2 held-out annotations.

**Co-I Powathil.** Mathematical guidance on landscapes, Fréchet means and the O2 well-posedness/stability targets; Standard Grant health-pathway framing (not an in-grant deliverable).

**Compute.** ~1,600 CPU-hours of the PI’s 5,000-hour Supercomputing Wales allocation; 1 Hz barcodes/landscapes; embarrassingly parallel by match. No additional HPC purchase.
