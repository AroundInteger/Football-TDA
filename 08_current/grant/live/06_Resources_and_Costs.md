## Justification (timeline)

**Research Associate (Months 5–10).** Specialist computational post for O1 scale-up and O2 implementation. Six months at 100% FTE is the delivery maximum under the £100k cap with PI 0.10 and Co-I 0.05.

- **Months 1–4 (PI):** runnable containerised pipeline; Month-2 cutoff gate and OSF pre-registration; O2 landscape/CUSUM specification so the RA inherits a working stack.
- **O1 (RA Months 5–9):** concurrent pipeline on Supercomputing Wales; full-season barcode database at Month 7 (D1). Comparison-geometry *results* (Month 9) are PI-led; the RA maintains the library they use.
- **O2 (RA Months 5–10):** landscape module at Month 8 (D2); support for T1/T2 statements and calibrated change-point outputs at Month 9; results finalised for the season paper at Month 10 (D3 handover).
- **Evidence pack:** RA handover at Month 10. Standard Grant *drafting* begins Months 9–10 with RA input (career development). The Month-12 Standard Grant pack is PI-owned.

**PI (0.10 FTE, Months 1–12).** Pipeline and Month-2 gate; O2 specification (Month 4); O1 comparison geometry (Months 6–9); theorem work and change-point calibration with RA implementation (Months 5–9); Month-10 handover; Month-12 evidence pack; publications (methodology Months 1–2; full-season results Month 11; football-analytics paper Month 12); partnership management; PDRA recruitment, supervision and training.

**Co-I Villamizar (0.05 FTE).** Applied algebraic and topological mathematics guidance for landscapes, Fréchet means and the O2 well-posedness/stability targets; structured input to RA training in algebraic/topological methods; co-supervision of Month-9 inference outputs.

**Compute.** ~1,600 CPU-hours of the PI’s 5,000-hour Supercomputing Wales allocation; 1 Hz barcodes/landscapes; embarrassingly parallel by match. No additional HPC purchase.
