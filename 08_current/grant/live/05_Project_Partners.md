# PROJECT PARTNERS

UKRI Funding Service: there is **no generic letter-of-support field**. An organisation appears only if it is entered in the Project Partner section, with prior agreement that, if the award is made, it will support the project as described. A formal collaboration agreement is then required for audit. Do not attach letters from the host or from Project co-lead research organisations.

Each partner record must (1) confirm commitment, (2) state the value, relevance and benefits of the work **to them**, and (3) describe the additional value **they bring**. Primary data route remains SCAFC; any second partner is backup, not a condition of O1–O2.

## Swansea City AFC (project partner; required)

- **Role:** Live co-development environment; practitioner interpretation of topological summaries as structural measures (pressing, formation gaps, defensive-line organisation).
- **Commitment:** In-kind access to Championship tracking and tactical labels through the club’s commercial data agreement; scheduled co-development reviews. No cash.
- **Value to them:** Checkable structural measures of pressing, formation gaps and defensive-line organisation, unavailable from length, width or hull area, developed against their matches and footage.
- **Value they bring:** The production tracking feed for O1–O2; formation and phase labels; coach- and analyst-facing validation that the summaries name real organisational states.
- **Status:** Secured. The V&A treats this agreement as the delivery route for O1–O2.

## Hudl (prospective project partner; not in place)

Hudl owns StatsBomb. Until Hudl agrees to be listed, StatsBomb remains a **data layer via SCAFC**, not a separate JeS partner. Do not add Hudl on the application, and do not imply they are committed, until a named contact with authority has agreed the three UKRI statements and the in-kind items below.

- **Role (if agreed):** Industry project partner for complementary shape features on tracking and video; optional independent research feed as backup to the SCAFC licence.
- **Commitment (proposed; no cash):** (a) named contact for the Project Partner section; (b) one or two short tactical/product reviews of overlays and named quantities; (c) if separately agreed, a research-use tracking feed (complete 22-player positions, consistent pitch model; 20-match validation batch or a Championship-equivalent subset). Raw tracks remain Hudl’s. If the award is made, a collaboration agreement covering data use, publication of methods and squad-level aggregates, and the in-kind items.
- **Value, relevance and benefits to them:** Hudl’s stack is video, events and value models (expected goals, On-Ball Value, 360 context). The project supplies a complementary, scale-separated organisation layer that can be overlaid on Hudl film and evaluated against geometric and 360 shape metrics. Peer-reviewed papers and open software give a citable, inspectable feature on tracking they already collect. Delivery of their product roadmap is not a project objective.
- **Additional value they bring:** Product and tactical-analysis expertise on StatsBomb data (solutions and product contacts); a second tracking source independent of a single-club licence, which reduces delay, coverage and licence risk; a path to test whether the features sit in existing analysis workflows. Not implementation of the method; not exclusive commercial rights; not named-player monitoring.
- **Status:** Exploratory (Jake Kolliari, Sr Solutions Consultant; Nick Glenday, Sr Product Manager; introduced September 2026). **Not secured. Do not name on JeS.**

## StatsBomb (data layer via SCAFC; not a JeS partner unless listed under Hudl)

- **Role:** Broadcast tracking and formation/tactical labels for the Championship season (~540 matches).
- **Contribution:** Positional tracking and label feeds used for the Month-2 cutoff gate, O1 comparison geometry, and O2 change-point annotation.
- **Status:** Secured through the Swansea City AFC commercial agreement. If Hudl becomes a project partner, this feed is described as Hudl/StatsBomb data accessed via SCAFC, plus any independent research feed Hudl commits in its partner record.
