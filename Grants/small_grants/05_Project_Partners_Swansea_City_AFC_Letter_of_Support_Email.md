Dear UKRI Research Council,

Swansea City Association Football Club is pleased to provide this letter of support for Dr Rowan Brown's research project on "Multi-Scale Topological Data Analysis of Competitive Collective Systems" at Swansea University.

**Commitment to the Project**

Swansea City AFC confirms its commitment to this 12-month research project and will support the work through:

- Provision of broadcast tracking data (10-25Hz) via our Statsbomb partnership, covering multiple Championship seasons
- Participation in quarterly project meetings (Months 3, 6, 9, 12) to provide tactical expertise and validation feedback
- Attendance at live demonstrations (Months 6, 11) and review of technical reports
- Entry into a formal collaboration agreement with Swansea University should funding be awarded, in accordance with UKRI audit requirements

**Value and Benefits to Swansea City AFC**

This research is highly valuable to our club for several reasons:

1. **Advanced Tactical Analysis**: The multi-scale framework (individual 3m, tactical 12m, team 30m scales) provides quantitative insights into team patterns. Preliminary results showing strong correlations (r=0.68, p<0.001) between topological features and attacking effectiveness suggest practical applications for performance analysis.

2. **Pattern Understanding**: The research will characterise how tactical patterns form and evolve, providing insights into pattern stabilisation timescales, stability regimes, and transition probabilities that can inform match preparation and in-game decision-making.

3. **Predictive Capabilities**: The project's objective to establish relationships between events and structural changes could enable early warning indicators of tactical transitions, enhancing our ability to anticipate opponent changes and optimise responses.

4. **Competitive Advantage**: Access to cutting-edge analytical methods positions Swansea City AFC at the forefront of data-driven football analysis in a rapidly growing £3.8 billion global sports analytics market.

**Additional Value We Bring**

Swansea City AFC contributes significant value to this research:

- **High-Quality Data**: Access to diverse Championship match data (multiple seasons, various team styles) essential for validating framework generalisability across 10-15 matches
- **Tactical Expertise**: Professional coaching and performance analysis staff provide domain knowledge to validate that detected patterns correspond to meaningful tactical structures and ensure practical relevance
- **Established Partnership**: Our track record of successful research collaboration with Swansea University, including supporting PhD projects, ensures efficient partnership establishment
- **Real-World Validation**: As a professional Championship club, we provide an authentic, high-stakes environment for validating the framework's robustness and practical applicability

**Conclusion**

Swansea City AFC is fully committed to supporting this research and believes it will deliver significant value to both the academic community and professional football. We look forward to a productive collaboration and confirm our commitment to enter into a formal collaboration agreement should funding be awarded.
