# EPSRC Mathematical Sciences Grant Application
## Detailed Budget Justification

**Total Full Economic Cost (fEC):** £450,000  
**Total Funding Applied For:** £360,000 (80% of fEC)  
**Total Contributions from Applying Organisations:** £90,000 (20% of fEC)

---

## STAFF COSTS (£320,000 - 71% of fEC)

### Principal Investigator (PI)
**Cost:** £135,000 over 36 months  
**Justification:** The PI will lead the theoretical development of the TDA framework, oversee all work packages, and manage industry partnerships. This includes 40% time allocation across all work packages, with particular focus on WP1 (Mathematical Framework Development) and WP4 (Industry Partnership and Commercialisation). The PI's expertise in TDA and dynamical systems is essential for the theoretical rigour required by EPSRC Mathematical Sciences.

**Breakdown:**
- Salary: £45,000/year × 3 years = £135,000
- Includes: Basic salary, employer's National Insurance, superannuation contributions

### Postdoctoral Research Associate (PDRA)
**Cost:** £105,000 over 36 months  
**Justification:** The PDRA will lead WP2 (Algorithm Implementation and Optimisation) and contribute significantly to WP3 (Multi-Match Validation Studies). This role requires expertise in computational topology, algorithm optimisation, and software development. The PDRA will develop the core algorithms for real-time topological analysis and ensure computational feasibility for industry applications.

**Breakdown:**
- Salary: £35,000/year × 3 years = £105,000
- Includes: Basic salary, employer's National Insurance, superannuation contributions

### PhD Student
**Cost:** £75,000 over 36 months  
**Justification:** The PhD student will focus on theoretical characterisation of soliton-like structures and contribute to WP1 and WP5. This role provides training opportunities whilst contributing novel theoretical insights. The student will develop mathematical proofs for soliton propagation in confined tactical spaces and contribute to academic publications.

**Breakdown:**
- Stipend: £18,000/year × 3 years = £54,000
- Tuition fees: £7,000/year × 3 years = £21,000
- **Total:** £75,000

### Technician (Data Processing Specialist)
**Cost:** £40,000 over 24 months  
**Justification:** The technician will support WP3 (Multi-Match Validation Studies) by managing data preprocessing, quality assurance, and computational infrastructure. This role is essential for handling large-scale GPS tracking data and ensuring data integrity across multiple matches.

**Breakdown:**
- Salary: £20,000/year × 2 years = £40,000
- Includes: Basic salary, employer's National Insurance, superannuation contributions

## RISK MITIGATION

### Budget Risks and Mitigation:
1. **Staff Recruitment Delays:** Contingency built into timeline, multiple recruitment rounds
2. **Equipment Cost Increases:** Fixed-price contracts where possible, alternative suppliers identified
3. **Travel Restrictions:** Virtual conference options, local collaboration prioritized
4. **Data Access Issues:** Multiple data sources, backup options secured
5. **Currency Fluctuations:** Equipment purchases in GBP where possible

### Value Protection:
- **Phased Spending:** Milestone-based releases ensure value delivery
- **Regular Reviews:** Quarterly budget reviews with adjustments as needed
- **Contingency Fund:** 5% contingency built into each category
- **Partnership Leverage:** Industry co-investment reduces risk exposure

This budget represents excellent value for money, delivering world-class research with significant academic, commercial, and societal impact while training the next generation of mathematical scientists.
