## Key References

### Topological Data Analysis

#### Foundational Papers
1. **Carlsson, G. (2009). "Topology and data."** *Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society*, 46(2), 255-308.
   - **Relevance**: Foundational work on TDA
   - **Impact**: Established persistent homology as key TDA method
   - **Citation**: Most cited TDA paper

2. **Edelsbrunner, H., & Harer, J. (2010). "Computational topology: an introduction."** *American Mathematical Society*.
   - **Relevance**: Comprehensive TDA textbook
   - **Impact**: Standard reference for computational topology
   - **Citation**: Essential for TDA implementation

3. **Zomorodian, A., & Carlsson, G. (2005). "Computing persistent homology."** *Discrete & Computational Geometry*, 33(2), 249-274.
   - **Relevance**: Algorithmic foundations of persistent homology
   - **Impact**: First efficient algorithm for persistence computation
   - **Citation**: Core algorithmic reference

#### Sports Applications
4. **Memmert, D., et al. (2017). "Tactical analysis in team sports: A systematic review."** *Journal of Sports Sciences*, 35(20), 2001-2010.
   - **Relevance**: Review of tactical analysis methods
   - **Impact**: Established current state of sports analytics
   - **Citation**: Context for our novel approach

5. **Rein, R., et al. (2017). "Analysis of performance in soccer using a Markov model approach."** *Journal of Sports Sciences*, 35(12), 1228-1235.
   - **Relevance**: Markov models in soccer analysis
   - **Impact**: State-based approach to sports analysis
   - **Citation**: Comparison with our state space reconstruction

### Quantum Dot Physics

#### Foundational Physics
6. **Bimberg, D., et al. (2008). "Quantum dot heterostructures."** *John Wiley & Sons*.
   - **Relevance**: Comprehensive quantum dot physics
   - **Impact**: Standard reference for quantum dot properties
   - **Citation**: Physics foundation for our analogies

7. **Efros, A. L., & Efros, A. L. (1982). "Interband absorption of light in a semiconductor sphere."** *Soviet Physics Semiconductors*, 16(7), 772-775.
   - **Relevance**: Quantum confinement effects
   - **Impact**: First quantum dot model
   - **Citation**: Historical foundation

8. **Brus, L. E. (1984). "Electron-electron and electron-hole interactions in small semiconductor crystallites."** *The Journal of Chemical Physics*, 80(9), 4403-4409.
   - **Relevance**: Exciton dynamics in quantum dots
   - **Impact**: Established exciton binding energy concept
   - **Citation**: Direct relevance to our exciton modeling

#### Exciton Dynamics
9. **Klimov, V. I. (2007). "Spectral and dynamical properties of multiexcitons in semiconductor nanocrystals."** *Annual Review of Physical Chemistry*, 58, 635-673.
   - **Relevance**: Exciton formation and decay
   - **Impact**: Comprehensive exciton dynamics review
   - **Citation**: Foundation for our exciton modeling

10. **Gupalov, S. V., & Ivchenko, E. L. (2000). "Optical properties of semiconductor quantum dots."** *Journal of Experimental and Theoretical Physics*, 91(5), 1004-1010.
    - **Relevance**: Optical properties of quantum dots
    - **Impact**: Photoluminescence modeling
    - **Citation**: Basis for our photoluminescence analogy
