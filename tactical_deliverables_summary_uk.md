## Risk Mitigation

### Academic Risks
- **Rejection from primary journal** → Have backup journals ready
- **Scooping by competitors** → File preprint to establish priority
- **Data access issues** → Use only processed results, not raw GPS data

### Commercial Risks
- **SecondSpectrum not interested** → Approach Stats Perform, Hudl, others
- **Patent rejected** → Rely on trade secrets and first-mover advantage
- **Market not ready** → Focus on grant-funded research projects first

### Hybrid Risks
- **Publication delays commercialisation** → Use patent pending period strategically
- **Commercial focus reduces academic productivity** → Set clear time boundaries
- **IP disputes** → Clear documentation and legal counsel from start

## Decision Framework

### Patent vs. Publish First Decision
**Patent First If**:
- Strong commercial interest from SecondSpectrum
- Sufficient budget ($25,000+)
- Willing to delay publication
- Want maximum IP protection

**Publish First If**:
- Academic credibility priority
- Open science values
- Limited commercial potential
- Want immediate academic impact

**Hybrid If**:
- Moderate budget ($10,000-20,000)
- Want both IP protection and academic impact
- Commercial interest exists but uncertain
- Want maximum flexibility

### SecondSpectrum Response Decision
**Strong Interest** → Negotiate partnership, delay paper publication if needed
**Moderate Interest** → Continue academic track, explore partnership in parallel
**No Interest** → Full academic route, approach other companies later

### Company Formation Decision
**Form Company If**:
- Want full control
- Willing to take risk
- Can commit time
- Have co-founder with commercial experience

**License If**:
- Want passive income
- Prefer academic focus
- Risk-averse
- Limited time commitment

**Neither If**:
- Commercial potential unclear
- Focus on academic impact
- Insufficient resources
- Prefer grant funding
