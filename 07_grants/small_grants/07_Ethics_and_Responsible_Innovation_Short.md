# ETHICS AND RESPONSIBLE INNOVATION

## Ethical and RRI Considerations

This research involves anonymised positional tracking data from professional football. Key considerations include data privacy, balancing open science with protection requirements, commercial application ethics, methodological transparency, and preventing dual use. RRI considerations encompass accessibility, environmental impact, societal benefit prioritisation, and stakeholder engagement.

## Management Strategy

**Data Privacy and Anonymisation**: All data is pre-anonymised by providers (Statsbomb, Genius Sports) using generic position labels, with no player names, jersey numbers, or personal characteristics. Data stored on secure, GDPR-compliant institutional infrastructure (100TB) with AES-256 encryption, role-based access controls, and secure transfer protocols. Data retained for project duration plus 7 years, then securely deleted. All outputs reviewed to prevent re-identification; aggregated statistics contain no identifiable information.

**Security**: Technical measures include encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, security audits, and encrypted backups. Organisational measures: data protection training, compliance with Swansea University Information Security Policy, access log monitoring, and incident response procedures.

**Data Re-use**: While raw tracking data cannot be publicly released due to commercial agreements, we release anonymised aggregated outputs (persistence diagrams, pattern signatures), comprehensive methodology documentation, open-source software (Python, MIT license), and detailed methodological descriptions in open access publications. Methods are designed for use with any compatible tracking data, enabling replication without requiring our specific dataset.

## Legal and Ethical Compliance

**Consent and Legal Basis**: Processing under legitimate research interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) and scientific research exemption (GDPR Article 9(2)(j)). Data collected by third-party providers with appropriate legal bases. Anonymised positional data requires no explicit consent. Transparency maintained through clear communication with data providers and publication of research objectives.

**Confidentiality**: Secure, access-controlled systems with project team bound by institutional data protection policies. No raw data sharing beyond project team. Aggregated outputs only in publications. Team-specific findings require partner permission; generic identifiers used where appropriate.

**Formal Information Standards**: Full compliance with GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, UKRI Data Policy, Swansea University policies (Information Security, Data Protection, Research Ethics, Research Data Management), and FAIR Data Principles where applicable.

## Responsible Innovation

**Open Science**: Open-source software release (GitHub), open access publications (UKRI block grants), and comprehensive methodology documentation. Research applications prioritise public safety (crowd management, emergency response), environmental benefits (traffic management), scientific advancement, and educational value.

**Environmental Sustainability**: Optimised algorithms minimise computational requirements. Efficient use of institutional HPC (shared infrastructure) and code optimisation reduce energy consumption. No dedicated hardware required.

**Dual Use Prevention**: Research focuses on understanding collective behaviour for positive applications, public safety, and scientific understanding. No development of tracking/surveillance technologies, individual identification, or privacy-invasive applications. Future commercial applications evaluated against public benefit, privacy protection, and transparency criteria.

**Ethics Approval**: Institutional ethics review through Swansea University Research Ethics Committee prior to project commencement. Fast-track approval expected (4-week turnaround) given anonymised pre-existing data, no direct human participant interaction, established protocols, and low-risk design. Ongoing quarterly review and compliance monitoring. Principal Investigator maintains overall responsibility with support from institutional committees and Data Protection Officer.
