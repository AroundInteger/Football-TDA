# Dan Morris - Swansea City AFC Data Scientist

**Dan Morris (Data Scientist, Swansea City AFC)**: Dan Morris serves as the Data Scientist at Swansea City AFC, responsible for all data pipeline processes, data engineering, and data analysis and dissemination to key stakeholders including coaching staff and board members. His expertise in sports data infrastructure, pipeline development, and translating analytical insights into actionable intelligence provides critical support for this research project. Mr Morris facilitates data access through the SCAFC-Statsbomb partnership, ensures data quality and compatibility with our analytical framework, and provides domain expertise for validating research findings. His role bridges academic research with practical applications, ensuring that topological patterns can be effectively communicated and applied within the club's tactical analysis processes.
