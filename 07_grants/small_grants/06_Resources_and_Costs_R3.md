## Justification of Resources

This section justifies the more costly resources and demonstrates how resources are comprehensive, appropriate, justified, and represent optimal use to achieve intended outcomes and maximise potential impacts.

### Project Staff

**Research Associate (9 months, 100% FTE, Months 2–10, £37,005)**

Essential for achieving all three research objectives and the strategic output within the 12-month timeframe. The 9-month appointment (Months 2–10) provides sustained specialist computational effort from pipeline setup through full-season analysis to persistence landscape development:

- **Objective 1 (Months 2–7)**: RA sets up concurrent processing pipeline on Supercomputing Wales and executes full-season analysis (~540 matches). Processing with persistent homology computation at 3 scales per frame, statistical validation, and quality control. Pipeline architecture enables simultaneous analysis of multiple matches, maximising computational efficiency.
- **Objective 2 (Months 4–8)**: Tactical fingerprinting requiring persistence diagram classification, formation metadata integration, and baseline signature identification for ≥3 tactical systems across the full season.
- **Objective 3 (Months 6–10)**: Persistence landscape computation, stability regime classification, and transition characterisation. Implementation of landscape distance metrics and temporal dynamics analysis.
- **Strategic Output (Months 9–10)**: Evidence synthesis for Standard Grant preparation, code documentation ensuring continuity.

**Optimal Resource Use**: The validated 11-match framework (Brown et al., 2026, ArXiv) eliminates methodological risk — the RA executes established methods at scale rather than developing new algorithms from scratch. PI analyses 15–20 initial matches (Month 1) before RA arrival, establishing the processing baseline. Mature open-source libraries (Ripser, GUDHI, giotto-tda) provide robust infrastructure without licensing costs. The 9-month appointment maximises specialist effort within the fEC cap, with PI coordinating Standard Grant preparation (Months 10–12).

**Maximising Outcomes**: Enables achievement of all three research objectives, production of full-season validated dataset, RA career development through co-authorship on Paper 3 (full-season results) and Standard Grant contribution, code documentation ensuring work continuation, and foundation for Standard Grant application.

**Principal Investigator (165 hours, £8,106)**

Critical for strategic direction and expertise that cannot be delegated: initial match analysis (Month 1) establishing processing baseline; RA supervision and methodology guidance (Months 2–10); industry partnership management (crucial for SCAFC–StatsBomb data access); persistence landscape methodology development; manuscript preparation (Paper 3, Months 10–12); and Standard Grant preparation (Months 10–12). 165 hours (average 3.2 hours/week) represents optimal balance, enabling essential oversight during RA appointment while maintaining other commitments and leveraging PI's established TDA expertise.

**Co-Investigators (66 hours total, £5,808)**

**Professor Liam Kilduff (33 hours, £2,904)**: Critical sports science expertise for interpreting topological patterns within professional football context, validating tactical fingerprinting outputs, and translating research findings into practical applications.

### Resource Optimisation Summary

The requested resources represent optimal use to achieve intended outcomes:

1. **Staff Allocation**: PI establishes baseline (Month 1) + 9-month RA executes full-season analysis and develops persistence landscapes (Months 2–10) + PI coordinates Standard Grant preparation (Months 10–12) + 2 Co-Is (66 hours) ensures comprehensive coverage across computational, domain, and mathematical expertise
2. **Reduced Methodological Risk**: The validated 11-match framework (ArXiv preprint) means the RA scales established methods rather than developing new ones, de-risking the investment
3. **Infrastructure Leverage**: Utilisation of existing institutional computational and storage infrastructure eliminates need for additional equipment costs
4. **Open-Source Tools**: Primary reliance on mature, free TDA libraries minimises software costs while maximising capability
5. **Partnership Value**: In-kind data access and expertise from Swansea City AFC provides significant additional value without direct cost

This resource structure ensures all three research objectives and the strategic output are achieved within the 12-month timeline while maximising potential outcomes, impacts, and value for money.
