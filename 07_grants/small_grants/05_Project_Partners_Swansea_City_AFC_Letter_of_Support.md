## Swansea City Association Football Club

**Re:** Letter of Support for Multi-Scale Topological Data Analysis of Competitive Collective Systems

Swansea City Association Football Club is pleased to provide this letter of support for Dr Rowan Brown's research project on "Multi-Scale Topological Data Analysis of Competitive Collective Systems" at Swansea University.

**Commitment to the Project**

Swansea City AFC confirms its strong commitment to this research project and will actively support the work through the provision of broadcast tracking data, tactical expertise, and validation support. We understand that this is a 12-month project analyzing 10-15 championship matches to develop novel mathematical frameworks for understanding competitive collective systems. We commit to:

- Providing access to broadcast tracking data (10-25Hz) through our established partnership with Statsbomb, covering multiple seasons and diverse team matchups
- Participating in quarterly project meetings (Months 3, 6, 9, 12) to provide tactical domain expertise and validation feedback
- Attending live demonstrations of the analytical framework (Months 6, 11) to ensure practical relevance
- Reviewing technical reports and findings to ensure analytical outputs align with real-world tactical applications
- Supporting the project team in interpreting results within the context of professional football tactics and performance

Should funding be awarded, we commit to entering into a formal collaboration agreement with Swansea University as required by UKRI audit procedures.

**Value, Relevance, and Benefits to Swansea City AFC**

This research is highly relevant and valuable to Swansea City AFC for several key reasons:

**1. Advanced Tactical Analysis Capabilities**
The project's multi-scale topological data analysis framework offers unprecedented insights into team tactical patterns at three validated scales: individual (3m), tactical (12m), and team (30m). Preliminary results detecting 523 distinct structural patterns with >95% statistical reliability, and demonstrating that tactical-level structures show 19% greater persistence in later match phases, suggest this methodology could provide quantitative, data-driven insights that complement our existing performance analysis. The multi-scale approach captures complementary information at each level — from individual player interactions to whole-formation structures — representing a significant advancement over traditional tactical analysis methods.

**2. Pattern Formation and Evolution Understanding**
The research objectives to characterise pattern formation from initial configurations and track pattern evolution will provide our coaching staff with deeper understanding of:
- How tactical structures emerge and stabilise during matches
- The timescales required for tactical patterns to form
- Stability regimes and transition probabilities between different tactical states
- Early warning indicators of tactical transitions that could inform in-game decision making

**3. Predictive Performance Insights**
The project's fourth objective to establish relationships between discrete events and structural changes, testing whether patterns predict system transitions, has direct application to match preparation and in-game tactical adjustments. If validated, these predictive capabilities could enhance our ability to anticipate opponent tactical changes and optimise our own tactical responses.

**4. Competitive Advantage in Data Analytics**
As the sports analytics market grows to £3.8 billion globally by 2025, access to cutting-edge analytical methods developed through this research positions Swansea City AFC at the forefront of data-driven football analysis. The open-source tools and methodologies developed will provide our analytics team with validated, scientifically rigorous methods for tactical pattern analysis.

**5. Long-term Strategic Value**
The research's pathway to a larger 36-month programme suggests ongoing collaboration opportunities that could yield increasingly sophisticated analytical tools. Our early involvement positions us to benefit from future developments in real-time computational tools, predictive modelling, and stability condition analysis.

**Additional Value Brought by Swansea City AFC**

Swansea City AFC brings significant additional value to this research project:

**1. High-Quality Data Access**
Through our established partnership with Statsbomb, we provide access to high-frequency broadcast tracking data (10-25Hz) covering multiple Championship seasons. This data diversity—encompassing various team styles, match contexts, and tactical systems—is essential for validating the framework's generalisability across 10-15 matches, directly supporting Objective 1 of the research.

**2. Professional Tactical Expertise**
Our coaching and performance analysis staff bring deep domain knowledge of professional football tactics, formations, and match dynamics. This expertise is crucial for:
- Validating that detected topological patterns correspond to meaningful tactical structures
- Interpreting results within the context of real-world football applications
- Ensuring analytical outputs are practically relevant and actionable for performance staff
- Providing feedback on the utility and interpretability of analytical methods

**3. Established Research Partnership Track Record**
Swansea City AFC has a demonstrated track record of successful research collaboration with Swansea University, including supporting PhD research projects. This existing relationship ensures efficient partnership establishment, clear communication channels, and mutual understanding of research objectives and constraints.

**4. Real-World Validation Context**
As a professional Championship club, we provide an authentic, high-stakes environment for validating the research framework. The competitive nature of our matches, the tactical sophistication of opponents, and the performance pressures inherent in professional football create an ideal testbed for ensuring the analytical methods are robust and practically applicable.

**5. Industry Network and Dissemination**
Our connections within the football industry, including relationships with other Championship clubs and football analytics organisations, can support broader dissemination of research findings and facilitate industry-wide adoption of validated analytical methods.

**Conclusion**

Swansea City AFC is fully committed to supporting this research project and believes it will deliver significant value to both the academic research community and professional football. We look forward to a productive collaboration that advances both scientific understanding of competitive collective systems and practical applications in football performance analysis.

Should you require any additional information or clarification regarding our commitment to this project, please do not hesitate to contact us.
