## ✅ **Changes Implemented**

### 1. **Budget Maximized to £100,000 FEC**

**Previous**: £80,000 FEC (£64,000 EPSRC)

**Rationale**: EPSRC Small Grants allow up to £100,000 FEC. Maximizing budget enables:
- Extended RA contract (9 months vs 6 months)
- More comprehensive analysis
- Better project delivery

**Budget Breakdown**:
- **Staff Costs**: £66,000

- **Equipment**: £8,000
- **Other Directly Incurred**: £11,000

- **Total**: £100,000

**Previous**: 6 months (Months 1-6)  
**New**: 9 months (Months 1-9)

**Benefits**:

- More comprehensive coverage of all work packages
- Better alignment with project timeline
- Enables parallel workstreams throughout project

**Updated Timeline**:
- **Months 1-3**: Multi-match validation (RA primary)
- **Months 4-6**: Event correlation framework (RA primary)
- **Months 7-9**: Temporal evolution analysis (RA primary, previously PI-only)
- **Months 10-12**: Synthesis and dissemination (PI primary)
