# EPSRC Mathematical Sciences Grant Application
## Pathways to Impact Plan

**Application Title:** Topological Data Analysis for Dynamic Team Behaviour: Mathematical Foundations for Sports Analytics

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This Pathways to Impact plan outlines how the research will deliver significant academic, industry, and societal benefits through systematic engagement with key stakeholders. The plan focuses on three primary impact pathways: (1) advancing mathematical sciences through novel TDA methods, (2) transforming sports analytics through industry partnerships, and (3) inspiring the next generation through educational outreach.

---

### Target Audiences

**Primary:**
- Topological data analysis research community

- Sports science and performance analysis academics

**Secondary:**

**Societal Risks:**
