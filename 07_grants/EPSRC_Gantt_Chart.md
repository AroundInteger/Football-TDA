## DEPENDENCIES AND CRITICAL PATH

### Critical Dependencies:
1. **WP1 → WP2:** Theoretical framework must be completed before algorithm implementation
2. **WP2 → WP3:** Algorithms must be optimized before large-scale validation
3. **WP3 → WP4:** Validation results required for commercial prototype development
4. **WP4 → WP5:** Commercial success enables broader impact and dissemination

### Risk Mitigation:
- **Parallel Development:** WP2 begins before WP1 completion to optimize timeline
- **Early Validation:** WP3 includes early validation phases to identify issues quickly
- **Multiple Pathways:** WP4 includes multiple industry partners to reduce risk
- **Continuous Dissemination:** WP5 activities begin early to maximize impact
