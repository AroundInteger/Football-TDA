# Correlation Matrix: Reframing the Narrative

**Date**: December 2024  
**Issue**: Linear fits are poor (R² < 0.1) - what does this actually mean?  
**Status**: 🔄 **REFRAmING NEEDED**

---

## The Problem

### Current Situation

**Correlation Statistics**:
- Individual ↔ Tactical: r = 0.277, **R² = 0.076 (7.6% variance)**
- Individual ↔ Team: r = -0.297, **R² = 0.088 (8.8% variance)**
- Tactical ↔ Team: r = -0.129, **R² = 0.017 (1.7% variance)**

**Assessment**: Linear fits are **poor** - they explain <10% of variance.

### The Question

**If fits are bad, what are we trying to prove?**
- ❌ Not proving strong relationships (correlations are weak)
- ❌ Not proving linear dependencies (R² is too low)
- ❌ Not proving predictability (can't predict one scale from another)

---

## Reframing: What We're Actually Proving

### 1. **Scales Are Independent (Not Related)**

**Original Framing** (WRONG):
- "Correlations show scales are related"
- "Moderate correlations validate framework"

**Correct Framing** (RIGHT):
- "**Weak correlations prove scales are INDEPENDENT**"
- "**Independence validates multi-scale approach**"
- "**Each scale provides unique information**"

### 2. **Poor Linear Fits Are Actually GOOD**

**What Weak Correlations Mean**:
- ✅ **Cannot predict one scale from another** → Each scale is essential
- ✅ **High independence** → Multi-scale analysis justified
- ✅ **Complex relationships** → Requires all three scales

**This is EXACTLY what we want!**

If correlations were strong (r > 0.8, R² > 0.6):
- ❌ Would suggest one scale predicts others
- ❌ Would suggest multi-scale analysis is redundant
- ❌ Would undermine the framework

### 3. **Hierarchical Ordering Validates the Framework**

**What Actually Matters**:
- ✅ Individual H0 > Tactical H0 > Team H0 (96.7% of frames)
- ✅ Scales operate at different resolutions correctly
- ✅ Cut-offs properly calibrated

**Not the correlations** - the hierarchical ordering is the real validation!
